**Reading the tables.**
- **H1.** On dev1 the P1 refinement step L1→L2 and the independent P4 offset are equal to two digits, for example −1.42e-3 and −1.42e-3 at AR-1. The observed order is 2.2 on dev1 and 2.0 on dev2. Both are consistent with second-order box integration converging onto the independent solver. The tolerances (2.4e-4 to 4.7e-3) are dominated by discretization; round-off (≤ 9.6e-10) and solver residual (≤ 1.5e-13) are negligible.
- **H-B.** Every Δ falls inside its band, near the geometric centre. The planning Laplace model of the substrate leakage (§2.1) predicts the body effect to within its ×/÷3 allowance. P4 reproduces every Δ to within 0.5 %.
- **P-DD.** The DEVSIM default drain current is unusable below G₀·1 mV ≈ 1e-10 A/cm. At ±1 mV its error ranges from 1.5e-2 to 70 in magnitude, largest at ψ̂ = 12; at ±0.1 mV it reaches 3.4e3. It is acceptable at AR-6: +2.5e-4 and +3.8e-4 at ±1 mV on levels 1 and 2, of which 2.44e-4 is the physical v²/6 term. In the same runs the circuit-node and weighting-function currents carry only the physical v²/6 term at ±1 mV. Their Richardson values agree with the network to ≤ 1.2e-9.
- **P-SS.** The coupled small-signal error reaches 7e3 relative while the backward residual stays at machine level; κ_G is 5e14 to 1.4e18. dev2 had no prediction: its errors are 23, 0.46 and 3.6e-3 at ψ̂ = 12, 16 and 21.

## Failure-mode atlas

Registered items: P-DD, P-SS, E-CIRC. Items marked *post hoc* were not pre-registered and decide nothing.

1. **Direct drain-side flux sum** (P1). This is the classical cancellation at a heavily doped contact (Polsky & Rimshans 1981; Palm & Van de Wiele 1985). On every A-R and HO mesh it misses the same-mesh reference by more than τ_num: by −3.1e3 (AR-1) and −4.0e3 (HO-5) at ψ̂ = 12, with the wrong sign, and still by −2.2e-4 at AR-6. The source side of the same solution is exact to round-off. The looser two-terminal B0 bracket misses it on 4 of 23 B-R meshes (dev2, ψ̂ = 16–21), where the error is smaller than the bracket. The three-terminal τ_num check flags it everywhere.
2. **DEVSIM post-solve contact current** (`get_contact_current`; P-DD).
   - Measured against the circuit-node current, its absolute error (worst of the four biases) is 1.5e-11 to 2.7e-11 A/cm on dev1 meshes and 2.4e-12 to 1.6e-11 A/cm on dev2 meshes, whatever the channel current.
   - *Post hoc:* on the dev1 meshes the raw drain and source values at ±0.1 mV are **bit-identical across gate voltages** whose G_DD spans 3.5e-12 to 2.1e-8 S/cm, so they carry no channel information. On dev2 the values differ between states but stay at the same magnitude.
   - On the dev1 meshes the source current is the same number at all four biases.
   - Newton converged to τ = 1e-12 V in every run, so no residual check would notice.
3. **Circuit-node current and weighting-function current** (E-CIRC). Both are accurate in every run. A plausible but unproven reason for the circuit current: in DEVSIM it is an unknown of the Newton system rather than a post-solve flux sum. **Practical rule:** at low current take terminal currents from a circuit node or a weighting function, never from `get_contact_current`.
4. **Coupled small-signal solve** (P-SS). Errors are O(1) to 10⁴ in deep subthreshold, while the backward residual stays at machine level.
5. *Post hoc:* **reference-form round-off.**
   - The two G_DD forms differ by up to 9.6e-10 (AR-1, level 2), while the G_SD forms agree to about 1e-14.
   - On the B0 network, every vertical cut through the source half of the channel agrees with −I_S to ≤ 1.5e-10, while the edge energy sits 1.1e-9 above (AR-1, level 2).
   - The excess grows with mesh level (1.0e-10 → 4.8e-10 → 1.1e-9) and with the edge-conductance range max c_e/G (5e17 at AR-1). At smaller ranges (≤ 1e16; AR-2, AR-4) both forms agree with the cuts to about 1e-10 and the diagnostic cannot separate them.

   **Recommendation for a future version** (not applied here): take G_DD's reference from −(I_S + I_B), or use the energy only as a check. The registered 1e-9 forms threshold is otherwise close to being limited by round-off on the finest dev1 mesh.

`benchmark/tools/export_cases.py`, extended by `tools/export_wp2.py`, exports 34 new cases. The 72 WP1 cases are byte-identical; the total is 106.

- **Meshes.** `benchmark/meshes/` publishes the node lines of the 7 reference meshes with their triangulation and contact rules. Each is cross-checked against all 46 P1 records (nx, ny, interface line, contact line, gate columns, contact extent).
- **Atlas entries.** Every output path of the project's own codes is attached to the case it estimates and classified mechanically:
  - a path computed on a published DEVSIM mesh is judged against the reference-path value on that same mesh (τ_num), so discretization is never counted as failure;
  - the independent P4 solver is judged against the established tolerance (a consistency check).

  Result in every A-R and HO case:
  - **known bad:** drain-side sums, DEVSIM drain and source contact currents, `G_E` and `G_M`;
  - **consistent:** second forms, circuit-node and weighting-function currents, P4.
- **Scorer.** It handles three-terminal cases: G_DD and G_SD scored; G_DB reported; τ_num on a declared `mesh_id`; tolerance for any mesh. A same-mesh submission on a coarser published mesh is scored on that mesh only. B-R cases accept G_DD or G_SD as G₀.
- **Worked examples.**

  | Example | Result |

  | DEVSIM drain contact current (same mesh) | 11/11 fail |
  | circuit-node current | 11/11 pass |
  | `G_E` | 11/11 fail |
  | independent FV | 6/6 pass |

- **Tests.** `pytest -q benchmark/tests` gives 28 passed: 15 WP1 tests, with the manifest count extended to the mesh files, and 13 WP2 tests. The WP2 tests cover:
  - counts that follow the registered evaluation;
  - mesh files against the cases and records;
  - τ_num discrimination (±5e-7 passes, ±2e-6 fails);
  - the section-7 tolerance rule;
  - every exported known-bad path failing and every cross path not failing;
  - B-R bracket scoring;
  - the presence of the atlas paths.

  The export is deterministic: two runs give an identical `MANIFEST.sha256`.

## Deviations and limits

- **Execution deviations** are A8 and A9 above. Three unsuccessful attempts are kept in the ledger, with their logs (`*.attempt1.log`).
- The A8 contingency for P2 runs that do not fit in memory was not needed: every registered run completed.
- **G_DB** follows the section-7 rule (2.2e-4 to 2.7e-3) but is reported, not scored, because the registered tolerance rule covers G_DD and G_SD only.
- **HO and B-R** have no established tolerance by design.
- **Internal paths only.** All agreement is between internal code paths: DEVSIM network, DEVSIM drift–diffusion, an independent finite-volume solver written for this project, and the B0 bracket. No external TCAD code has been scored, and nothing here is experimental validation.
- The **physics envelope** is narrow: Boltzmann statistics, constant mobility, holes excluded from the observable, no generation/recombination, V_D → 0.
